Frequently Asked Questions

Everything about categories, swiping, retrieving, and how the AI checks availability.

At signup you tell us which sizes count as "baggy" for you and which count as "fitted" — for example, baggy might be L–XXL while fitted is S–M. These personal categories drive the AI assistant's availability checks.
Drag the top card left or right. Right swipes land on your Liked page where you can purchase them; left swipes go to your Rejected page. You can also use the heart and X buttons below the deck.
The free plan includes 3 retrieves — each one puts a rejected piece back into your swipe deck. Upgrading to Pro removes the limit entirely so you can second-guess yourself as much as you like.
Ask it about any piece — e.g. "Is the Kyoto Heavyweight Hoodie available in baggy XL?" It verifies the fit against your categories, checks size overlap and stock, and even compares the garment's chest measurement to your width.
